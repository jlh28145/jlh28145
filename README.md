## 👋 About Me

I’m Von, a software quality engineering leader with hands-on experience across test automation, CI/CD, embedded systems, Android platforms, simulation, and system-level validation.

My work sits at the intersection of **quality strategy and software engineering**. I focus on building reliable systems that help teams detect risk earlier, investigate failures faster, and release software with greater confidence.

My approach to quality engineering emphasizes:

- Automation architecture that scales with the product
- Deterministic and maintainable test design
- CI/CD-integrated validation and quality gates
- System-level debugging and artifact collection
- Risk-based testing and release readiness
- Practical tooling that improves engineering efficiency

> Testing should be treated as an engineering system, not a checklist.

**Highlights**

- Command-line workflow
- Automated output organization
- Faster defect investigation and test review
- Designed around real QA debugging workflows

```text
Collect useful evidence when failures occur.
Measure risk, not merely test counts.
Design tools around real engineering workflows.
```
